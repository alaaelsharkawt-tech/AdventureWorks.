The dashboard includes KPIs and advanced calculations such as:
* Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin %
* Total Returns & Return Rate %
* Time Intelligence: Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth & Year-over-Year (YoY) Performance
* Actual vs. Target Performance Analysis
* Customer Lifetime Value & Top 100 Customer Segmentation
* Dynamic Pricing & Scenario Sliders

The measures were designed to respond dynamically to slicers, bookmarks, and dynamic visual filters.
